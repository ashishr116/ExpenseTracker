# Components

Small reusable pieces in `Views/Components/`.

- **`ExpenseRow.swift`**: one expense line with its category icon, title, payment method, date and amount.
- **`CardPaymentRow.swift`**: a credit card bill payment in the Daily and Monthly lists. Shown in grey and never added to any total.
- **`MonthSelector.swift`**: the "‹ October 2026 ›" control for stepping between months.
- **`MessageComposeView.swift`**: the system Messages composer, plus the sheet that shows it. On devices that cannot send texts it falls back to Share and Copy.
- **`RupeeGlyph.swift`**: the drawn ₹ symbol used by the splash screen and app icon.
